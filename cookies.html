Cookie Policy

This page describes the browser storage and cookie-like technologies used by DADTrack on its public website and authenticated workspace.

1. What this policy covers

Cookies are small files placed in a browser. “Cookie-like technologies” can also include local storage or session storage. This policy should be read with our Privacy Policy.

Current position: DADTrack does not intentionally use advertising cookies, cross-site ad pixels, or non-essential analytics cookies on the public website.

2. Essential browser storage

DADTrack may use browser storage that is necessary for an authenticated session, account activation, navigation state, or the login introduction experience. These values help the application remember the state of the browser and do not create an advertising profile.

Authentication stateUsed to keep an authorized session available in the browser after sign-in.
Interface preferenceUsed for short-lived UI state, such as whether the sign-in introduction has been viewed.
Security contextUsed by the authentication or application flow to validate and protect requests.
Operational dataWorkspace data is requested from the application and connected APIs; it is not stored as an advertising cookie.

3. Fonts and third-party requests

Pages may request the Be Vietnam Pro font from Google Fonts so the public experience uses the intended brand typography. The browser may connect to Google’s infrastructure to retrieve that font. You can review Google’s own terms and privacy information for how that request is handled.

Authenticated workspaces can also make requests to Supabase or other configured infrastructure and to connected advertising or MMP APIs. Those services process requests under their own policies and the permissions configured for the workspace.

4. What we do not use

  • No advertising cookie is used by DADTrack to follow you across unrelated websites.
  • No non-essential cookie is used to sell or broker personal information.
  • No third-party ad pixel is intentionally installed on the public landing page under the current configuration.

5. Your browser controls

You can delete or block cookies and storage through your browser settings. Blocking essential storage may prevent sign-in, account activation, or parts of the workspace from working correctly. If you use a shared device, sign out and clear browser data when appropriate.

6. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the website, authentication flow, or integrations change. The current version is published on this page with its update date. Questions can be sent to nghiadt2702@gmail.com.
